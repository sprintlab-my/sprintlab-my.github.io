# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

SprintLab Sdn Bhd is committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all. We expect all members, partners, and contributors to adhere to this Code of Conduct.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to creating a positive environment include:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment and discrimination
- Disrespectful or insulting language
- Unwelcome sexual advances or attention
- Publishing others' private information
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported by contacting the community team. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly.
